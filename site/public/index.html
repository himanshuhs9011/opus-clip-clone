Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything runs on your own computer, so there are no server costs to pass on. No subscription, no credits, no watermark.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That warning appears for new apps that aren't code-signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Which AI does it use?

By default Ollama, a free AI that runs on your PC; setup installs it for you. You can also add an OpenRouter key to use cloud models, or use the built-in rules with no AI at all.

Does it work without an NVIDIA graphics card?

Yes. Transcription then runs on your processor, so it takes longer. Everything else works the same.

Which videos can I clip?

Your own uploads, or YouTube links. Only clip videos you have the rights to use.
