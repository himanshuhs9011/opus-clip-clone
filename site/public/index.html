Ranked before you watch

Every clip gets a score out of 100, with the reasons behind it and the risks to fix. A second AI pass compares the clips, so weak ones never reach your feed.

Game streams, cut like streamers do

Four steps, no account

  1. Install

    Run the setup and click Next. It fetches the AI and video tools itself.

  2. Add a video

    Drop a file or paste a YouTube link. Links download only the parts it needs.

  3. Review

    Read the ranked clips, fix a caption, trim, change the style.

  4. Export

    Save ready-to-post 1080 by 1920 MP4s, one by one or all at once.

Captions
Eight animated styles, keyword colours and a different colour for each speaker.
Framing
Follows whoever is talking, splits two-person shots and stacks a face-cam over gameplay.
Editing
Cut words from the transcript, remove silences and filler, trim on a timeline.
Your PC
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. An NVIDIA card makes it fast, any PC works.

Why it isn't online yet

Cutting video needs a graphics card for every upload. Online, that means renting GPUs by the hour, and they are hungry for money I don't have yet.

So for now ClipForge runs on your computer, on the card you already own, and stays free. When I can pay for the servers, I will put it online.
